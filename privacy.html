Privacy Policy — ComoEmpezoTodo

Last updated: November 7, 2026

ComoEmpezoTodo is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to publish his own videos to his own TikTok account.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization via TikTok Login Kit, the App accesses basic profile information (display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and uses the video upload/publish permissions to post the owner's videos.

Storage

The access token is stored only locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server operated by the App, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Other users

The App does not collect any data from other TikTok users.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); the local token is then deleted.
